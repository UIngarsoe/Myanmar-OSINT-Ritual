An open-source stylistic classification and generative engine designed to inject the legendary narrative prowess of **Thawda Swe (သော်တာဆွေ)** and **Khin Myo Chit (ဒေါ်ခင်မျိုးချစ်)** into modern Large Language Models (LLMs). Built to drive the **SSISM Intel Wizar Heroes 34-Day Publishing Series**.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Literary DNA

This engine combines two distinct pillars of classical Burmese storytelling:

1. **Thawda Swe (သော်တာဆွေ) - The Realist & Satirist**:
   * Colloquial Burmese narrative tone (စကားပြောဟန်).
   * Unpretentious, direct, and humorous character dialogue.
   * Deeply humanized historical figures with realistic flaws and emotions.

2. **Khin Myo Chit (ဒေါ်ခင်မျိုးချစ်) - The Epic Historian**:
   * Majestic, epic narrative framing (ခမ်းနားထည်ဝါသော သမိုင်းဖွဲ့ဆိုမှု).
   * Rich cultural and historical authenticity.
   * Structured saga architecture based on *Heroes of Old Burma*.
